# First experiment: ReviewGate

Build a small review tool for AI-assisted client briefs, not a broad AI compliance platform. The buyer hypothesis is an operations lead at a 5–50-person bookkeeping or advisory firm who needs to know which person approved the exact client-facing draft. This is a research-backed experiment, not an investible-business verdict.

## Why this problem first

[Menlo Ventures' September 2026 consumer AI report](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2026-the-state-of-consumer-ai/) puts accuracy (45%), trustworthiness (40%), and security/privacy (36%) ahead of ease of use (32%) among AI users' choice factors. Its July survey covered 5,067 U.S. adults, weighted to be nationally representative. The publisher is an investor with commercial interests; the consumer findings do not establish a small firm's purchasing intent.

The [U.S. Chamber's 2025 small-business report](https://www.uschamber.com/technology/empowering-small-business-the-impact-of-technology-on-u-s-small-business) says 58% of surveyed small businesses use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024. [OECD's December 2025 SME report](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/12/ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises_9c48eae6/426399c1-en.pdf) identifies skills, data, finance and privacy/security barriers. Together these suggest a workflow worth testing; they do not show that this tool has a budget owner.

The difficult engineering is preserving a trustworthy relationship between generated text, a human decision, and the exported version. Sensitive-data detection is another hard problem, but the prototype only demonstrates heuristic redaction. It must not be presented as comprehensive privacy protection. [GAO's May 2026 review](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107828) corroborates inaccurate-output and privacy/security risks in a government context, not SME demand.

## The alternatives are close

The scoring command gives ReviewGate 64/100, invoice exceptions 63/100, and content approvals 55/100. These are subjective prioritization scores with a one-point gap between the first two—not meaningful statistical evidence of superiority. ReviewGate wins this first build because it can demonstrate a coherent offline approval boundary without access to accounting systems or customer accounts.

Invoice exception handling addresses clearer financial pain: [Intuit's 2025 report](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-late-payments-report-2025/) says 56% of its surveyed businesses were owed unpaid invoices, averaging $17,500. But [QuickBooks already provides automatic reminders](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/invoicing/send-invoice-reminders-automatically-manually/L84cQjpxo_US_en_US); differentiation would depend on reliable cross-system reconciliation, not generating reminder copy.

[Verizon's 2025 SMB survey](https://www.verizon.com/about/news/2025-state-small-business-survey) says 54% struggle to produce enough fresh content. Content approvals are feasible, but channel distribution is crowded and Menlo's report also finds consumer resistance to known AI-generated content. A generator alone is a weak initial bet.

## The competitive risk is real

[Microsoft Purview already offers AI-related data classification, DLP, auditing, and retention capabilities](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/ai-microsoft-purview). ReviewGate should not compete on breadth or make compliance claims. The only plausible initial wedge is a simpler, workflow-specific review experience for firms whose current tools are a poor fit. Whether that wedge exists must be learned directly from buyers.

## What would justify investing more

Interview 10–15 target firms. Ask to see a recent real draft review, the time spent, the error it caught, the current tool, and who can approve a purchase. Seek at least two paid 30-day pilots before adding integrations. A proposed pilot price is a testable commercial assumption, not a reported market rate; agree it with actual buyers rather than inventing revenue projections.

Measure review time, rejected drafts, changes after approval, and repeated weekly use. Stop or change niche if there is no recurring problem, no budget owner, or bundled tools already solve it. Do not collect confidential client material during discovery without appropriate consent and handling arrangements. These U.S./OECD sources do not establish Indian demand; validate the chosen geography explicitly.
