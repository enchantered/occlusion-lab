# Decision: Keep ReviewGate validation narrow; no new build

**Decision (2026-10-19):** Continue only the existing ReviewGate experiment and its buyer-validation gate. Do not start invoice-exception or subscription-leak builds. New evidence strengthens the pain case—especially late invoices—but still does not establish willingness to pay for any proposed product. This is a validation decision, not product-market fit, investment readiness, or a security claim.

## Ranked comparison

1. **Invoice exceptions — 67/100; strongest broad pain, not a build authorization.** [QuickBooks' July 7, 2026 report](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-late-payments-report-2026/) says 59% of surveyed small businesses had invoices 30+ days overdue (versus 47% last year), with $17.7K average outstanding among those owed. It also says 26% found digital tools useful for chasing/collecting. This improves the pain evidence, but the report is not a product-buying test. [BILL's current AR plans](https://www.bill.com/product/pricing) already bundle invoice tracking, reminders, payment tools and accounting sync for $49–$89/user/month; exact exception reconciliation still brings integration and reliability burden. Consider only if interviews establish a distinct, recurring exception workflow buyers cannot solve in their accounting tools.

2. **ReviewGate — 64/100; retain as the only active hypothesis, with modest evidence.** [Goldman Sachs' March 17, 2026 report](https://www.goldmansachs.com/community-transformation/10000-small-businesses-voices/insights/ai-presents-a-major-opportunity-for-small-businesses) describes a survey of 1,256 program participants fielded Jan 27–Feb 4: 76% used AI, 50% of users cited privacy/security concerns, 49% technical-expertise gaps, and only 14% fully integrated AI into core operations. The sample is a selected cohort, not representative proof of demand for review software. The [OpenAI Agents SDK documentation](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/human_in_the_loop/) describes native approvals and durable pause/resume state, making basic approval mechanics easy to copy. The proposed differentiation remains the narrow, auditable link between a human and the exact client-brief version approved—not a broad compliance claim—and buyer value is unproven.

3. **Subscription leak detection — 46/100; no build.** [CNET/YouGov's July 6, 2026 report](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/subscription-survey-2026/) says its weighted online survey of 2,522 U.S. adults estimated $252/year in unused subscriptions. That's reported waste, not willingness to pay. [Rocket Money's current feature page](https://www.rocketmoney.com/feature/manage-subscriptions) already advertises recurring-subscription detection and cancellation assistance. Incremental savings, bank-data access, trust, and repeat payment remain unproven.

The comparable scores and fuller evidence are in the [opportunity ranking JSON](2026-10-19-opportunities.json). Scores are explicit analyst hypotheses under `venturelab/opportunities.py`, not probabilities. Invoice exceptions rises from 63 to 67 solely because demand is scored 5 rather than 4; no new evidence justifies better feasibility, differentiation, distribution or payment scores. A three-point lead is not statistical evidence of superiority. It makes invoice exceptions the leading discovery candidate, not a validated business. Sources listed with publication dates use dates stated in the source; current competitor/SDK observations with no stated publication date are separately recorded with `observed_on` in the JSON. Older reports are retained only as context, not presented as new. No standalone current consumer-preference report changes the prior evidence; the focused updates are the 2026 SMB survey, QuickBooks late-payments report and CNET subscription survey.

## Next gate and maintenance

Keep the buyer gate at **10–15 interviews and at least two paid pilots** for one client-brief workflow before integrations or scope expansion. No outreach is authorized here. Stop or change niche if pain is not recurring, a budget owner cannot be identified, or bundled tools suffice. **Indian demand remains unvalidated.**

The maintenance increment adds portable audit checkpoints. A retained trusted witness can detect a rollback that still passes the ordinary internal audit, while allowing later valid events. It does not provide automatic external anchoring, signatures, authenticated identity, encryption or an export gate. The actual SDK remains pinned and is tested through its real Runner with a synthetic offline model; no live model-provider call was made. New transaction regressions cover competing approval decisions and fault-injected rollback, not OS power-loss recovery. Full validation and commit details are recorded with the final delivery.
