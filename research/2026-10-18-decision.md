# Decision: Continue ReviewGate; defer adjacent MVPs

**Decision (2026-10-18):** Continue the existing ReviewGate experiment; do not start another MVP. This is a maintenance-and-validation decision, not evidence that product-market fit or willingness to pay exists. The product hypothesis remains a lightweight record tying a human reviewer to the exact approved client-brief version—not a compliance suite or comprehensive privacy tool.

Scores are analyst judgments under `venturelab/opportunities.py`, not probabilities: weights are pain 25%, demand 20%, payment likelihood 20%, feasibility 15%, differentiation 10%, distribution 10%; each input is 0–5 and weighted total divided by five. One point between ReviewGate and invoice exceptions is not meaningful evidence of superiority. The model's eligibility check verifies only presence of demand and engineering evidence, not their quality, independence, geography, freshness, or commercial validation. Competitor pricing is not a proxy for add-on willingness to pay: Canopy's current $74–$149/user/month accounting workflow plans are not evidence of demand for ReviewGate. The OpenAI Agents SDK's built-in human-in-the-loop approvals make a simple approval log especially easy to copy, not a moat.

## What changed / what did not

Maintenance work reproduced the original SQLite default file mode of `0644` even under `umask 0`. New databases now use `0600`; newly created missing directories use `0700`; symlinks and permissive existing database files are rejected. Database contents remain plaintext, so this is a restrictive-permissions mitigation, not encryption or comprehensive data protection.

The actual OpenAI Agents SDK 0.24.0 `Runner` path was tested offline with a fake model: typed output and tracing-off behavior work, no tools are enabled, inputs are redacted, and drafts remain pending approval; invalid drafts are not saved. No provider/model API calls were made and no model-evaluation budget was spent. This establishes a narrow technical path only, not production readiness or a security certification.

## Gate before expansion

Conduct 10–15 interviews with target U.S. firms and seek **at least two paid pilots** for one client-brief workflow before adding integrations or widening scope. Establish recurring review pain, a budget owner, and what current bundled tools fail to do. No outreach is authorized by this decision. Indian demand remains unvalidated. Stop or change niche if pain is not recurring, no buyer can fund a pilot, or existing tools adequately solve the workflow.
